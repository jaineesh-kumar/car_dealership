Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

At Marlow Motors, we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard the data you provide to us through our website and dealership services.

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with our website, such as submitting an enquiry, booking a test drive, or requesting a valuation, we may collect:

  • Personal details (Name, phone number, email address)
  • Vehicle preferences and current vehicle details
  • Technical data (IP address, browser type, device information) through cookies

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the collected information solely for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your specific requests and enquiries
  • To schedule test drives and showroom appointments
  • To provide accurate valuations for trade-in vehicles
  • To improve our website's functionality and user experience

3. Data Protection

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party marketers. Your data is only shared with trusted partners when strictly necessary to fulfill your requests (e.g., financing partners), and only with your explicit consent.
