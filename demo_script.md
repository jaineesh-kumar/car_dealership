# Marlow Motors Demo Script
**Duration:** ~3 minutes
**Goal:** Show the dealer exactly what they get and how easy it is to manage. 
*Always run the demo on a mobile phone (or Chrome mobile emulator) to highlight the responsive design!*

---

### Part 1: The Pitch & First Impression (0:00 - 1:00)

**[Open the homepage on mobile]**
"Welcome to the new digital showroom for Marlow Motors. Car buyers today do 90% of their research on their phones before they ever visit the lot. We built this platform from the ground up to load instantly and look premium—like an Apple product launch, not a crowded classifieds site."

**[Scroll slowly through the homepage]**
"Notice the clean, dark cinematic theme. Everything is designed to make the cars the absolute focus. You have your Featured Inventory directly on the homepage, and we clearly outline your key selling points: the 120-Point Inspection, the warranty, and transparent pricing."

### Part 2: Browsing & The Car Detail Page (1:00 - 2:00)

**[Tap on the 'Inventory' button in the navbar]**
"Let's look at the inventory. It's incredibly fast. Users can instantly filter by brand or fuel type. Let's select one of these cars."

**[Tap on a specific car (e.g., a BMW or Audi)]**
"This is the detail page. Notice the high-quality image gallery they can swipe through. We've got quick specs at the top, a full description, and a list of features. 
But here is the most powerful tool for your customers: **The EMI Estimator**."

**[Adjust the EMI Estimator sliders]**
"They can dynamically adjust their down payment and loan term right here to see exactly what their monthly payments would look like. It keeps them engaged on your site longer."

### Part 3: Lead Generation & Admin (2:00 - 3:00)

**[Scroll to the 'Interested?' form]**
"Once they are sold on a car, generating a lead is completely frictionless. They just fill out this quick form—they can request a general enquiry, a callback, or explicitly book a test drive."

**[Submit a test lead on the form]**
"When they hit send, this doesn't just go to an email inbox where it can get lost. It goes straight into your private management portal."

**[Switch over to the Django Admin Panel (`/admin`)]**
"This is your dealership management system. From here, you can see every single lead that comes in. 
But more importantly, this is how you add new inventory. It's so simple that anyone on your team can do it."

**[Click 'Add' next to Cars]**
"You just enter the car details, and at the bottom, you can instantly upload up to 6 photos directly from your phone. You can add a brand-new car to the website in under two minutes flat."

---

### Closing (Phase 2 Mention)
"This is Phase 1. It gives you a beautiful, high-converting digital showroom today. In Phase 2, we can look at adding online booking payments and automated WhatsApp notifications for your sales team. What do you think?"
